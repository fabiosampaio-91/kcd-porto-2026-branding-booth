## Content

The deck has three pillars, framed by three objectives (brand exposure, business development, hiring):

1. **The Booth**: the "Stand KCD" spot on Piso -1 sits at the venue entrance. Reuse last year's backdrop with a printed cover strip ("synvert xgeeks, a Synvert GL Company"), new TV frame and table front panel, bigger footprint (two tables, possibly two screens), Instax corner and games.
2. **Branding across the venue**: every unsold sponsor asset becomes ours. Side room naming, xgeeks Lounge (Piso 0), wifi name and password, lanyards, barista coffee corner with pun rollup, "Sponsor room brought to you by synvert xgeeks" foyer sign, water bottles.
3. **CTO's Dinner**: the official welcome dinner on day one, sponsored by Synvert xgeeks.

The final slide lists the open decisions the team needs to close, with owners to be assigned.
