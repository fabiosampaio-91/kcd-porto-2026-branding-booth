Pillar 01 | Booth

Reuse smart, rebrand cheap

Last year's booth is a strong base. We update it instead of buying everything new

1 cover strip
2 new TV frame
3 new front panel
Our booth at KCD Porto 2025
  • 1. Reuse the backdrop. We print a cover strip over the part that says "synvert" and place the new identity there: synvert xgeeks, a Synvert GL Company
  • 2. New TV frame. Make it new and cheaper, with the new logo and design
  • 3. New table front panel. Same approach: new print, new design, low cost
  • Everything else (table, TV, hardware) we already own
Principle: spend on presence and dynamics, not on rebuying structures that still work.

Booth dynamics: give people a reason to stop

Traffic is guaranteed by the location. Engagement is up to us

📸 Instax corner

Instant camera with a branded xgeeks photo frame. We post the photos on our social channels and tag the people in them, so they reshare and amplify us to their own networks.

🎮 Games for giveaways

Quick games at the booth with merch as prizes. Last year's raffle worked. This year we can go bigger: a wheel of fortune, a leaderboard challenge, or a claw-style pick.

🎁 Merch and giveaways

Tiered swag: cheap high-volume items for everyone, plus a few desirable prizes that make people play and stay.
